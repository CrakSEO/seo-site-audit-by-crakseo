Use before publishing or after a migration to check up to 5 supplied URLs per audit, or inspect a page's speed. This is a focused SEO Tools subset, not an automatic whole-site crawler. Distinguish Lighthouse lab checks from field measurements.

- How do I compare technical SEO issues on up to five pages? Use `run_seo_site_audit`.
